A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A little clarity.

What can I save in the beta?

Version 0.2.2 adds supported Instagram, X, YouTube, Pinterest, and TikTok post links alongside direct HTTPS MP4, JPEG, PNG, and WebP links. Earlier store beta builds support direct media links only. Availability depends on the source provider; TikTok photo slideshows and restricted posts may be unavailable.

Can I use my library offline?

Once a file finishes downloading, its local copy is available for viewing or playback without downloading it again. Adding new media needs an internet connection.

What happens when I delete a download?

ClipShare removes the entry and its local media copy. Copies you already exported to Photos, Gallery, Files, or another app remain there until you delete them separately.

Is ClipShare available to everyone?

ClipShare is currently in beta. Public App Store and Google Play downloads are not available yet. Contact us below to ask about testing access.
