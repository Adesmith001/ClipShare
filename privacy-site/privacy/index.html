ClipShare: Media Library

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 24, 2026

Your media library stays on your device. Here is how ClipShare handles links, downloads, and information you choose to share.

Who this policy covers

Toluwani Somade develops ClipShare: Media Library (also displayed as ClipShare Beta). This policy covers the store beta for Android and iOS: a personal media library that saves direct HTTPS video and image links. The Android store beta uses package name com.adesmith001.clipshare.beta. This beta does not include social-post resolution or social-account sign-in.

Information handled on your device

ClipShare processes the links you paste or explicitly share to the app so it can download the media you request. It stores downloaded photos and videos, source links, available titles and thumbnails, download status and error codes, and library preferences on your device. This information supports playback, search, sorting, download recovery, and export.

ClipShare does not require a ClipShare account. The store beta does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, or an analytics SDK, and does not automatically send your library or diagnostic reports to the developer.

Network requests and media hosts

When you request a download, your device connects to the media URL and any hosts used to deliver or redirect that request. Those hosts receive the requested URL, your IP address, and standard connection information needed to serve the file. A URL may itself contain identifying information or access tokens; only submit links you intend to use for a download.

Media is downloaded to your device, without passing through a ClipShare-operated server. The media host's privacy policy governs its handling and retention of requests. The store beta does not send links to a third-party social-post resolver.

Permissions and exports

ClipShare uses internet access for requested downloads. When you choose to save or export an item, it uses the platform's Photos, Gallery, Files, or sharing features and requests permission where required. An export or share sends the selected item to the location or app you choose; that service's own privacy practices apply. Denying Photos access or cancelling an export leaves the copy in ClipShare available.

Diagnostics and support

You can choose to copy a diagnostic report and send it with feedback. It includes the app version and build, operating-system information, local storage totals, entry counts and states, recovery counts, and error codes. The generated report excludes media URLs, titles, folder names, and account or session data. Copying a report places it on the system clipboard; it does not send it to the developer.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or reports you choose to provide. We use them to respond and investigate the issue. Avoid including private media or credentials. Feedback sent through TestFlight or an app store is also handled by that platform under its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Your local library remains until you delete items or remove the app's data. Delete a download in ClipShare to remove its library entry and local media copy. On Android, you can also clear the app's storage through system settings. Removing the app normally removes its app-managed data; device or cloud backups may retain earlier copies according to your backup settings.

Copies exported to Photos, Gallery, Files, or another service remain there until you delete them separately. Device and cloud backups are controlled by your operating system and chosen provider.

Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve and follow up on your request. You can request deletion by emailing the contact below. We will delete correspondence no longer needed, except where retention is necessary to meet a legal obligation or resolve a dispute. Mail-provider backups may persist according to that provider's retention practices.

Sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Support email is processed by our email provider. Information may also be disclosed when required by law. Requested media downloads use HTTPS, and local files rely on your device's security protections. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

This privacy website

This page uses no cookies, analytics scripts, or contact forms. It is hosted on Vercel, which may process technical request information such as IP address, browser information, requested page, and access time to deliver and protect the website. Hosting logs are handled under Vercel's privacy policy and the hosting service's retention settings.

Children's privacy

ClipShare is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information through support. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its revision date when data practices change. A release that adds social-platform features or other data handling will require an updated policy describing those features.

Contact and privacy requests

For questions, support, or requests about personal information you have sent to us, contact Toluwani Somade at somadetoluwani@gmail.com. We do not have remote access to your device's local library.
